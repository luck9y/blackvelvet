Enforcement Framework
This document explains staff expectations and the three-tiered enforcement model: Level 1 — Warnings; Level 2 — Demotion & timeout; Level 3 — Permanent removal.
Level 1 — Warning
Behaviors that receive a formal warning
Warnings are recorded. Multiple warnings lead to Level 2 review.
  1. Failure to follow staff guidance during events Documented warning and coaching. Repeated incidents escalate to demotion review.
  2. Unprofessional language toward members Verbal correction and written warning. Continued behavior leads to Level 2 action.
  3. Minor misuse of staff channels or resources Warning and instruction on proper use; monitoring for recurrence.
  4. Neglecting assigned duties without notice Warning and requirement to provide explanation; habitual neglect escalates.
  5. Poor conflict handling with members Mediation and a formal warning; training recommended.
  6. Sharing non-sensitive internal notes publicly Warning and reminder of confidentiality expectations.
  7. Late arrival to scheduled staff responsibilities Warning and scheduling review; repeated lateness escalates.
  8. Inadequate moderation decisions (first instance) Review and coaching; written warning if decision caused harm.
  9. Failure to log incidents properly Warning and retraining on reporting procedures.
  10. Minor policy misunderstandings Clarification and a formal warning; staff training provided.
Level 2 — Demotion & Timeout
Behaviors that lead to demotion and temporary removal
Demotion removes staff privileges. Timeouts are temporary suspensions from staff duties while corrective steps are taken.
  1. Repeated Level 1 violations Demotion to a lower role and a timeout period for review and retraining.
  2. Abuse of staff powers (moderation overreach) Immediate demotion, incident review, and temporary suspension from duties.
  3. Deliberate favoritism or nepotism Demotion and timeout while leadership investigates and documents corrective steps.
  4. Sharing sensitive staff-only information publicly Demotion and temporary removal pending investigation; possible further action.
  5. Repeated failure to follow event protocols Demotion and timeout from event duties; retraining required before reinstatement.
  6. Moderate harassment of members or staff Demotion and temporary suspension; mediation and documented corrective plan.
  7. Using staff channels for personal gain Demotion and timeout; restitution or corrective measures may be required.
  8. Repeated poor moderation decisions causing community harm Demotion and temporary removal from moderation duties; review of decision-making process.
  9. Evading accountability or obstructing investigations Demotion and timeout while leadership completes a formal review.
  10. Unauthorized use of staff accounts or credentials Demotion, temporary suspension, and security review; credentials revoked until cleared.
Level 3 — Permanent Removal
Behaviors that result in permanent removal from staff
Level 3 incidents are severe and typically result in immediate termination of staff privileges and possible reporting to authorities.
  1. Deliberate doxxing or publishing private information Immediate permanent removal and incident report to platform or authorities as required.
  2. Sharing explicit content involving others without consent Immediate permanent removal and mandatory reporting where applicable.
  3. Theft of accounts, credentials, or funds Immediate permanent removal and notification to affected parties and platforms.
  4. Coordinated harassment or brigading organized by staff Immediate permanent removal and cross-community notification if necessary.
  5. Extortion, blackmail, or coercion of members Immediate permanent removal and escalation to law enforcement when warranted.
  6. Distribution of malware or facilitating illegal activity Immediate permanent removal and reporting to platform security teams.
  7. Sexual exploitation or abuse involving minors Immediate permanent removal and mandatory reporting to authorities.
  8. Repeated evasion of sanctions combined with malicious behavior Permanent removal of all accounts and network-wide enforcement where possible.
  9. Severe, targeted hate campaigns led by staff Immediate permanent removal and public notice to affected communities when appropriate.
  10. Any action that creates legal liability for the clan Immediate permanent removal and legal escalation as required.
All enforcement decisions are documented. Staff under review will be notified and given an opportunity to respond. Leadership retains final authority on demotions and removals. For Level 2 and Level 3 actions, at least two senior leaders must approve the outcome.

Investigation workflow

Reports are handled consistently: intake, evidence collection, staff review, decision, and documentation. Preserve logs and screenshots. Leadership will coordinate interviews and collect statements when needed.

Reporting expectations

When filing a report, include timestamps, screenshots, chat logs, and a concise summary. Submit reports to the private staff incident channel or contact leadership directly for urgent matters.

Demotion and timeout details

Demotion removes staff permissions and access to staff channels. Timeouts include a clear reinstatement plan with training or probation conditions. Reinstatement requires documented completion of corrective steps.

Appeals

Appeals must be submitted in writing to leadership within seven days of the action and should include new evidence or context. Leadership will respond and document the outcome.
