Staff Rules & Enforcement

Standards and enforcement for staff conduct — clear, fair, and consistent.

Enforcement Framework
Three-level model
Level 1 — Warning; Level 2 — Timed removal; Level 3 — Permanent removal.
Applied with evidence and context
Investigation workflow: Notice → Evidence collection → Decision → Enforcement notice. Staff must document timestamps, screenshots, and relevant logs when reporting incidents.
Investigation details
Investigations are led by senior staff. Evidence is collected, reviewed, and stored. Decisions include corrective steps, temporary removals, or permanent removal depending on severity and history. Appeals instructions are below.
Level 1
Warning
Behaviors that result in a formal warning; coaching and corrective guidance.
Typical actions: warning, coaching
1
Failure to assist during assigned duty; first instance results in a warning and coaching.
2
Unprofessional language toward teammates; documented warning and guidance.
3
Misuse of internal staff channels for non-staff matters; reminder and warning.
4
Neglecting assigned duties without notice; warning and corrective plan.
5
Accidental sharing of low-sensitivity staff info publicly; remove and warn.
6
Repeated minor mistakes after coaching; escalate if pattern emerges.
Level 2
Extended, Timed Removal
For repeated or more serious violations; temporary loss of staff privileges.
Typical actions: temporary suspension, role locks
1
Repeated Level 1 violations after warnings and coaching.
2
Abuse of staff powers (improper moderation actions); temporary removal and review.
3
Behaviors causing harm to teammates or community members; suspension and mediation.
4
Using staff channels for personal gain or unauthorized sharing of sensitive info.
5
Repeated poor moderation decisions causing community harm; corrective suspension.
6
Evading prior punishments using alternate accounts; temporary removal and investigation.
Level 3
Permanent Removal
Severe violations that terminate staff membership and may be reported externally.
Typical actions: permanent removal, reporting
1
Repeated disregard of prior enforcement levels and corrective steps.
2
Sharing explicit or illegal content through staff channels.
3
Misuse of accounts, credentials, or sensitive data leading to harm.
4
Harassment, threats, or discrimination by staff toward members or staff.
5
Actions that create legal liability for the community or platform.
6
Coordinated malicious campaigns or distribution of malware/illegal content.
Enforcement is applied with context and evidence. Leadership will communicate updates to staff. Appeals and reporting details are in the sidebar.

Investigation workflow

Notice → Evidence collection → Decision → Enforcement notice. Document timestamps, screenshots, and logs.

Reporting expectations

Reports must include timestamps, context, and supporting evidence. Incomplete reports may be delayed.

Demotion & removal

Demotion and removal decisions are final. Temporary removals may be appealed after 30 days with new evidence.

Appeals

Submit appeals in writing to leadership@domain.com within 30 days of enforcement notice. Include evidence and a clear explanation.

Quick reference

Level 1: Warning, coaching required.
Level 2: Timed removal, corrective steps.
Level 3: Permanent removal, reporting as needed.
