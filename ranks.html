Overview

Ranks reflect contribution, conduct, and community standing. Each rank below is presented as a short article so you can understand expectations and privileges.

Ranks are awarded by staff and reviewed periodically. Progression depends on activity, behavior, and contributions. Use these descriptions to understand what each rank represents and how to move up.

GOAT — Singular Leadership

The highest honor, reserved for a single member who exemplifies leadership and long-term contribution.

About GOAT

GOAT is awarded by Founders to a single member who consistently demonstrates exceptional leadership, mentorship, and positive impact. This rank is symbolic and carries community recognition and responsibility.

Privileges: Public recognition, priority for event leadership, and a direct line to Founders. Expectations: Model behavior, mentor others, and represent the community externally.

ELITE — Top-tier Mentors

Top-tier players who lead by example and mentor others.

About ELITE

ELITE members are recognized for skill, leadership, and consistent mentoring. They help run training sessions, lead teams, and support competitive events.

Privileges: Mentor channels, event leadership opportunities. Expectations: High conduct standards and active mentoring.

LEGEND — Respected Veterans

Veterans who have contributed over time and are pillars of the community.

About LEGEND

LEGEND recognizes long-term contributors who consistently support the community. They are reliable, experienced, and often help organize events and initiatives.

Privileges: Recognition and priority for certain events. Expectations: Maintain positive conduct and support community initiatives.

DECENT — Active Contributors

Active members who participate regularly and contribute positively.

About DECENT

DECENT members are steady contributors who participate in events and help newer members. They are known for constructive involvement and reliability.

Privileges: Contributor channels and event invites. Expectations: Continue participating and help newer members when possible.

ROOKIE — New Recruits

New members learning the community and improving their skills.

About ROOKIE

ROOKIE is for new recruits who are learning the rules and culture. Rookies are encouraged to ask questions, participate, and follow guidance from Helpers and Moderators.

Privileges: Access to basic channels and events. Expectations: Learn the rules, be respectful, and engage constructively.

MEMBER — Community Foundation

The baseline rank for community participants who keep the server active.

About MEMBER

MEMBER is the standard rank for participants. Members form the foundation of Black Velvet and are expected to follow rules and contribute positively.

Privileges: Full access to general channels. Expectations: Follow rules and contribute positively to the community.
