Clan Rank Information

Every member, visitor, and challenger fits somewhere in this structure. These ranks define skill, dedication, and your place within our community.

RANK DIRECTORY

Read carefully — your journey starts here. To try out you must open a ticket.

Image
GOAT
The highest rank in the entire clan. Only one player can ever hold this title. THE BEST OF THE BEST — the one who proved themselves above every other competitive player. If you want this rank, you’ll have to take it in a 1v1.
Exclusive. Competitive challenge required.
Image
ELITE
Absolute beasts in the game. Insane mechanics, terrifying aim, unmatched movement — dominate lobbies without breaking a sweat.
Image
LEGEND
Strong, reliable, and respected. Not pro level, but consistently performs and stands out above average players.
Image
DECENT
Solid players who can hold their own in public matches. Understand the game, make smart decisions, and survive without needing backup.
Image
ROOKIE
New players or those still learning the basics. Struggles in solo matches and needs guidance — the beginning of your climb.
Image
MEMBER
People who join the server to be part of the community. Chill, talk, play, and vibe — no pressure, no requirements.
TO TRYOUT YOU MUST OPEN A TICKET!
Want to climb the ranks? Open a tryout ticket in Discord and follow the instructions. Consistency, attitude, and performance matter. Tryouts are scheduled by leadership and may require recorded matches or live 1v1s.
Need help?

Questions about ranks, tryouts, or placement? Reach out in the staff channel or open a ticket in Discord.
