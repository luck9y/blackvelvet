Welcome to the Black Velvet Clan Staff Directory

This section explains the roles that keep the server organized, safe, and evolving. Each position carries responsibility — together they form the backbone of the community.

F
FOUNDERS
The creators and builders of the clan.
Founders focus on development, adding new features, fixing bugs, and improving the server’s systems. They design architecture, set long-term direction, and handle high-level partnerships and integrations. Founders are the architects behind everything that makes Black Velvet run smoothly.
P
PRESIDENT
Leaders in charge of the entire server.
Presidents oversee operations, make executive decisions, and ensure the clan’s vision stays strong. They handle major updates, partnerships, community direction, and final approvals on policy changes. Presidents coordinate with founders and admins to keep the server healthy and growing.
A
ADMIN
Managers who keep everything functioning correctly.
Admins supervise staff, handle escalations, and ensure the server stays organized and active. They bridge leadership and moderation, manage server settings, coordinate events, and support moderators with complex cases.
M
MODS
The Moderators of the server.
Moderators enforce rules, manage tickets, and keep the community safe from troublemakers. They monitor chat, resolve disputes, apply sanctions when necessary, and escalate incidents to admins. Moderators are the watchers of Black Velvet — they make sure the server is safe and welcoming.

H
HELPER
Friendly guides of the clan.
Helpers assist players with questions, direct them to the right channels, and help train newcomers. They provide onboarding, explain server systems, and support tryouts or practice sessions. Helpers are the first point of contact for new members.
Staff members of Black Velvet serve entirely on a voluntary basis and agree to uphold all server rules, act with maturity, and maintain professionalism. By accepting any staff role you acknowledge these guidelines may change at leadership’s discretion and that violations may result in warnings, demotions, removal, or bans. All staff must be 17 years or older. Anyone found to have lied about their age will be permanently banned from the server.

CLAN RANK INFORMATION

Welcome to the official rank directory of the Black Velvet Clan. Every member, visitor, and challenger fits somewhere in this structure. These ranks define skill, dedication, and your place within our community. Read carefully — your journey starts here.

G
GOAT
The highest rank in the entire clan. Only one player can ever hold this title. THE BEST OF THE BEST — the player who proved themselves above every other competitive player. To earn this rank you must challenge and win a 1v1 against the current GOAT under leadership-supervised conditions.
Exclusive. Formal challenge and verification required.
E
ELITE
Elite players are absolute beasts in the game. Insane mechanics, terrifying aim, unmatched movement — they dominate lobbies without breaking a sweat. Elites are often invited to competitive scrims and represent the clan in high-level matches.
L
LEGEND
Legends are strong, reliable, and respected. Not necessarily pro level, but consistently perform and stand out above average players. Legends are often leaders in casual squads and help newer players improve.
D
DECENT
Decent players are solid and can hold their own in public matches. Not flashy, but capable — they understand the game, make smart decisions, and survive without needing constant backup.
R
ROOKIE
Rookies are new players or those still learning the basics. They struggle in solo matches and need guidance — this rank is the beginning of your climb. Training and mentorship are encouraged.
M
MEMBER
Members are community participants who join to chill, talk, play, and vibe. No competitive requirements — just be part of the space. Members can try out for competitive ranks by opening a ticket.
TO TRYOUT YOU MUST OPEN A TICKET!
Want to climb the ranks? Open a tryout ticket in Discord and follow the instructions. Consistency, attitude, and performance matter. Tryouts are scheduled by leadership and may require recorded matches or live 1v1s.
Questions about roles or ranks? Reach out to leadership in the staff channel.
