Staff — Who Runs Black Velvet

Staff are the backbone of the community. They set standards, resolve disputes, and keep the server safe and welcoming. Below you’ll find a concise, article-style breakdown of each staff tier with the current members listed prominently.

Staff roles are organized by responsibility and scope. Founders and Presidents provide leadership and direction. Admins handle technical and policy enforcement. Moderators manage day-to-day community interactions. Helpers focus on onboarding and member support. Each role has expectations and a clear chain of accountability.

If you need to contact staff, use the staff channels in Discord or open a ticket. For privacy-sensitive matters, message a Founder or President directly.

Founders — Vision & Governance

Founders set the long-term direction and core values of Black Velvet. They make final decisions on major policy, partnerships, and community structure.

Founders are responsible for the community's identity and long-term health. They approve major changes, resolve escalated disputes, and ensure the server's vision remains consistent. Founders are expected to act transparently, document major decisions, and mentor senior staff.

The President coordinates staff, runs operations, and ensures policies are enforced consistently.

The President acts as the primary operational leader. Responsibilities include scheduling staff, coordinating major events, and serving as the primary point of contact between Founders and the staff team. The President also leads high-level incident reviews and ensures staff training and onboarding are effective.

Clan Ranks — What They Mean

Ranks reflect contribution, skill, and community standing. Each rank has expectations and privileges — read the short articles below to understand how to progress.

Clan ranks are awarded based on activity, conduct, and contributions. They help identify leaders, mentors, and active members. Ranks are awarded by staff and reviewed periodically. Below are the rank descriptions presented as readable, article-style entries so you can understand the purpose and expectations for each level.

GOAT — Singular Leadership

The GOAT rank is the highest honor. It is reserved for a single member who demonstrates exceptional leadership, consistent positive impact, and a long history of contributions. The GOAT is selected by Founders and may be changed only by unanimous Founder decision.

Privileges: Special recognition, priority for event leadership, and a direct line to Founders. Expectations: Model behavior, mentor others, and represent the community publicly.

ELITE — Top-tier Mentors

ELITE members are top-tier players and mentors. They consistently demonstrate skill, leadership, and a willingness to help others. ELITE members often lead training sessions, mentor rookies, and help organize competitive events.

Privileges: Event leadership opportunities, mentor channels. Expectations: High conduct standards, active mentoring, and consistent participation.

LEGEND — Respected Veterans

LEGEND recognizes respected veterans who have contributed over time. These members are reliable, skilled, and often serve as community pillars. LEGEND status is awarded for sustained positive impact.

Privileges: Recognition and priority for certain events. Expectations: Maintain positive conduct and support community initiatives.

DECENT — Active Contributors

DECENT members are active contributors with a positive reputation. They participate regularly, help in events, and are known for steady, constructive involvement.

Privileges: Access to contributor channels and event invites. Expectations: Keep participating and help newer members when possible.

ROOKIE — New Recruits

ROOKIE is for new recruits learning the community. Rookies are encouraged to participate, ask questions, and follow guidance from Helpers and Moderators. This rank is the first step toward higher roles.

Privileges: Access to basic channels and events. Expectations: Learn the rules, be respectful, and engage constructively.

MEMBER — Community Foundation

MEMBER is the baseline rank for community participants. Members keep the server active and form the foundation of Black Velvet. Good conduct and participation are the keys to moving up.

Privileges: Full access to general channels. Expectations: Follow rules and contribute positively.
