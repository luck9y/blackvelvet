Staff Directory & Clan Ranks — Full

Managing the Black Velvet Clan and its members across all ranks.

Staff Roles

F

Founders

Original creators and vision keepers. Oversight of all operations and long-term direction.

P

President

Manages administrative operations, staff coordination, and community engagement.

A

Admin

Handles technical tasks, server maintenance, and enforcement of policies.

M

Moderator

Monitors chat, assists members, and enforces community guidelines.

H

Helper

Assists new users, answers questions, and helps maintain a welcoming environment.

Clan Ranks

G

GOAT

Highest rank. Only one holder at a time. Requires Founder/Admin verification.

E

ELITE

Top-tier players known for skill and leadership; mentors to others.

L

LEGEND

Respected veterans recognized for mastery and loyalty.

D

DECENT

Active contributors with a positive reputation in the community.

R

ROOKIE

New recruits learning the ropes and growing within the clan.

M

MEMBER

Everyday participants who form the foundation of the community.

Want to climb the ranks? Open a formal ticket in Discord and follow the instructions provided.
Questions about roles or ranks? Reach out to Admins for clarification.
