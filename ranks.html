Welcome to the Black Velvet Clan Staff Directory

This section explains the roles that keep the server organized, safe, and evolving. Each position carries responsibility — together they form the backbone of the community.

Image
FOUNDERS
The creators and builders of the clan.
Founders focus on development, adding new features, fixing bugs, and improving the server’s systems. They are the architects behind everything that makes Black Velvet run smoothly.
Image
PRESIDENT
Leaders in charge of the entire server.
Presidents oversee operations, make executive decisions, and ensure the clan’s vision stays strong. They handle major updates, partnerships, and community direction.
Image
ADMIN
Managers who keep everything functioning correctly.
Admins supervise staff, handle issues, and make sure the server stays organized and active. They bridge leadership and moderation, maintaining balance and order.
Image
MODS
The Moderators of the server.
Moderators enforce rules, manage tickets, and keep the community safe from troublemakers. Moderators are the watchers of Black Velvet — they make sure the server is safe.

Image
HELPER
Friendly guides of the clan.
Helpers assist players with questions, direct them to the right channels, and help train newcomers. They can train or redirect players and explain what they need to know.
Staff members serve on a voluntary basis and agree to uphold all server rules, act with maturity, and maintain professionalism. By accepting any staff role you acknowledge these guidelines may change at leadership’s discretion and that violations may result in warnings, demotions, removal, or bans. All staff must be 17 years or older. Anyone found to have lied about their age will be permanently banned from the server.
CLAN RANK INFORMATION

Welcome to the official rank directory of the Black Velvet Clan. These ranks define skill, dedication, and your place within our community. Read carefully — your journey starts here.

Image
GOAT
The highest rank in the entire clan. Only one player can ever hold this title. THE BEST OF THE BEST — the one who proved themselves above every other competitive player. If you want this rank, you’ll have to take it in a 1v1.
Exclusive. Competitive challenge required.
Image
ELITE
Absolute beasts in the game. Insane mechanics, terrifying aim, unmatched movement — dominate lobbies without breaking a sweat.
Image
LEGEND
Strong, reliable, and respected. Not pro level, but consistently performs and stands out above average players.
Image
DECENT
Solid players who can hold their own in public matches. Understand the game, make smart decisions, and survive without needing backup.
Image
ROOKIE
New players or those still learning the basics. Struggles in solo matches and needs guidance — the beginning of your climb.
Image
MEMBER
People who join the server to be part of the community. Chill, talk, play, and vibe — no pressure, no requirements.
