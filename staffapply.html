Staff Application

Apply to join the staff team. Complete all fields and be honest — leadership reviews every submission.

Provide the exact tag so we can verify your account. Applicants must meet the minimum age requirement for staff roles.
By submitting you confirm the information is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

What we look for

Reliability, clear communication, fair judgment, and a willingness to learn. Experience is valued but not required for every role.

Process

Applications are reviewed by leadership. If selected, you'll be invited to an interview and a short probation period. Expect a response within 7–14 days.

Privacy

Application details are visible only to leadership and senior staff. Sensitive incidents are handled through private channels.

Need help?

Contact leadership in the staff channel or message a senior staff member directly.
