STAFF RULES

Staff represent Black Velvet at all times. These standards apply to every staff member, regardless of rank.

01. Be professional.

Remain calm, respectful, and fair. Do not use your role to intimidate, embarrass, or win personal arguments.

02. Never abuse permissions.

Use moderation tools only when there is a valid reason. Do not punish friends or enemies differently, and never use powers for entertainment.

03. Document serious actions.

Record important moderation actions with clear reasons and relevant evidence whenever possible. Transparency protects both staff and members.

04. Keep internal matters private.

Do not leak staff chats, applications, reports, private information, or leadership discussions outside authorized spaces.

05. Escalate when needed.

If a situation exceeds your permissions or knowledge, contact a higher-ranking staff member instead of guessing or acting recklessly.

06. Stay active and accountable.

Communicate absences, complete assigned responsibilities, and accept feedback. Staff rank is a responsibility, not a status symbol.
