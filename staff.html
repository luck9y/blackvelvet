STAFF DIRECTORY

👑 OWNER
The creator, the leader, the final voice. The owner can do whatever he wants — full control over the clan, server, and direction.
🛠️ MANAGER
Ensures the Discord and game are functioning properly. Fixes bugs, designs layouts, oversees staff behavior, and keeps everything running smoothly.
⚖️ ADMIN
Handles tickets, gives roles, and makes final decisions (except the owner’s). Ensures players feel safe, resolves conflicts, and can host events for the community.
🛡️ MODERATOR
Secures the server. Bans cheaters, punishes rulebreakers, manages tickets, and follows the ticket guide. Moderators keep the community clean and fair.
🤝 HELPER
Assists players, answers questions, and supports moderators. Sends helpful messages like “Please wait, moderators are busy,” guides players to the right channels, welcomes new members, and bumps the server. Helpers are the friendly face of the clan.
