Staff Rules
Community Standards
Thirty rules organized by severity. Follow them to keep the community safe and fun.
Level 1
Minor Infractions
Warnings and short mutes for first-time or low-impact issues
  1. Trolling or light provocation First response: verbal warning. Repeats: short mute.
  2. Spamming chat or emoji floods Remove content and issue a warning; temporary mute if repeated.
  3. Posting off-topic content in focused channels Content moved or removed; reminder about channel purpose.
  4. Mild harassment or rude remarks Warning and request to apologize; short mute for escalation.
  5. Excessive profanity in general chat Tone-down request; repeated use may lead to a temporary mute.
  6. Ignoring staff instructions (first instance) Warning and clarification of expectations.
  7. Minor cheating or exploiting (low impact) Warning and temporary restrictions; review for repeat behavior.
  8. Poor sportsmanship during matches Warning and possible short suspension from events.
  9. Unauthorized advertising once Link removed and a warning issued.
  10. Confusing impersonation Request to change display name; warning if intentional.
Level 2
Moderate Infractions
Stronger action for repeated or more harmful behavior
  1. Repeated targeted harassment Multi-day mute or temporary suspension; staff review.
  2. Sharing partial personal information Immediate suspension pending investigation; escalate if malicious.
  3. Posting explicit content in public channels Content removed and temporary ban depending on severity.
  4. Cheating that affects matches Temporary ban from events and review of account status.
  5. Threatening language or intimidation Temporary suspension and staff mediation.
  6. Repeated advertising after warnings Temporary ban and link removal.
  7. Evading punishments with alternate accounts Account suspension and possible permanent ban.
  8. Persistent identity-based harassment Temporary suspension and mandatory mediation.
  9. Large-scale copyright infringement Content removal and temporary suspension.
  10. Deliberate disruption of events Event disqualification and temporary ban.
Level 3
Severe Infractions
Immediate and decisive action; possible reporting to authorities
  1. Publishing private personal information Immediate permanent ban and incident report to platform or authorities.
  2. Sharing explicit images without consent Immediate permanent ban and mandatory reporting where required.
  3. Credible threats of violence Immediate permanent ban and escalation to law enforcement if necessary.
  4. Hate speech targeting protected groups Immediate permanent ban and removal of content.
  5. Account theft or credential harvesting Immediate permanent ban and notification to affected users.
  6. Coordinated harassment campaigns Network-wide bans and reporting to other communities.
  7. Distribution of malware or illegal content Immediate permanent ban and report to platform security teams.
  8. Sexual exploitation of minors Immediate permanent ban and mandatory reporting to authorities.
  9. Extortion or blackmail Immediate permanent ban and escalation to law enforcement.
  10. Persistent evasion combined with malicious behavior Network-wide enforcement and permanent removal from community.
Enforcement is guided by evidence and context. Staff will review reports, request supporting material, and apply penalties consistently. Appeals are accepted through the official appeals channel.

Key points to remember

Respect other members. Staff decisions are final during active incidents. Preserve screenshots and report issues to the staff channel.

Reporting and appeals

To report a violation, include timestamps, screenshots, and a short description. For bans or suspensions, submit an appeal with evidence and a clear explanation. Appeals are reviewed within a reasonable timeframe.

Event conduct

Players must follow event rules and staff directions. Match disputes should be reported immediately with evidence. Unsportsmanlike conduct may result in removal from events.

Staff contact: use the staff channel for urgent issues. For privacy-sensitive incidents, message a staff member directly.
Enforcement policy updated for clarity. Questions about these rules can be directed to the leadership team.
