BLACK VELVET // COMMUNITY PROTOCOL

RULES

These rules keep Black Velvet fair, safe, and enjoyable. Joining or participating in the community means you agree to follow them.

01. Respect everyone.

No harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted hostility, or unnecessary drama. Treat members, visitors, and staff with basic respect.

02. No cheating or exploits.

Do not use cheats, hacks, exploits, or intentionally abuse bugs. Report issues to staff instead of using them for an advantage.

03. Keep communication clean.

Do not spam, flood channels, advertise without permission, impersonate others, or post harmful and inappropriate content.

04. Follow staff direction.

Staff decisions must be respected during active situations. If you disagree with a decision, raise it calmly through the correct channel afterward.

05. Protect the community.

Do not share private information, internal content, staff discussions, or anything intended to harm Black Velvet or its members.

06. Use common sense.

Not every situation can be listed here. Attempts to bypass the spirit of these rules may still result in staff action.
