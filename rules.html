Black Velvet Clan Rules

Clear, fair, and enforced. Read before joining events or discussions.

Level 1
Minor Infractions
Warnings and short mutes for first-time or low-impact issues.
Typical actions: warning, short mute
1
Trolling or light provocation
First response: verbal warning. Repeats: short mute.
2
Spamming chat or emoji floods
Remove content and issue a warning; temporary mute if repeated.
3
Posting off-topic content in focused channels
Thread moved or removed; warning issued.
4
Mild harassment or rude comments
Warning and removal of content.
5
Excessive profanity in general chat
Tone-down request; repeated use may lead to a temporary mute.
6
Ignoring staff instructions (first instance)
Warning and clarification of expectations.
7
Minor cheating or exploiting (low impact)
Warning and temporary restrictions; review for repeat behavior.
8
Poor sportsmanship during matches
Warning and possible short suspension from events.
9
Unauthorized advertising or links
Link removed and a warning issued.
10
Confusing impersonation
Request to change display name; warning if intentional.
Level 2
Moderate Infractions
Stronger action for repeated or more harmful behavior.
Typical actions: temp ban, suspension, staff review
1
Repeated personal harassment
Temporary ban or suspension; staff review.
2
Sharing partial personal information
Immediate deletion and warning; escalate if malicious.
3
Posting explicit content in public channels
Content removed and warning issued.
4
Threatening language or intimidation
Temporary suspension and staff mediation.
5
Repeated advertising after warnings
Temporary ban and link removal.
6
Evading punishments with alternate accounts
Account suspension and permanent ban.
7
Persistent identity-based harassment
Temporary suspension and mediation.
8
Large-scale copyright infringement
Content removal and temporary suspension.
9
Deliberate disruption of events
Event disqualification and temporary ban.
Level 3
Severe Infractions
Immediate and decisive action; possible reporting to authorities.
Typical actions: permanent ban, reporting
1
Publishing private personal information
Immediate permanent ban and incident report to platform or authorities.
2
Sharing explicit images without consent
Immediate permanent ban and mandatory reporting where required.
3
Credible threats of violence
Immediate permanent ban and escalation to law enforcement if necessary.
4
Hate speech targeting protected groups
Immediate permanent ban and network-wide enforcement.
5
Account theft or credential harvesting
Immediate permanent ban and notification to affected users.
6
Coordinated harassment campaigns
Network-wide bans and reporting to other communities.
7
Distribution of malware or illegal content
Immediate permanent ban and report to platform security teams.
8
Sexual exploitation of minors
Immediate permanent ban and mandatory reporting to authorities.
9
Extortion or blackmail
Immediate permanent ban and escalation to law enforcement.
10
Persistent evasion combined with malicious behavior
Network-wide enforcement and permanent removal from community.
Enforcement is guided by evidence and context. Staff will review reports, request supporting material, and apply penalties consistently. Appeals are accepted through the official appeals channel.

Key points to remember

Respect other members. Staff decisions are final during active incidents.
Preserve evidence. Save screenshots and timestamps when reporting.
Report clearly. Include timestamps, screenshots, and a short description.

Reporting and appeals

To report a violation, include timestamps, screenshots, and a short description. For bans or suspensions, submit an appeal with evidence and a clear explanation. Appeals are reviewed within a reasonable timeframe.

Event conduct

Players must follow event rules and staff directions. Match disputes should be reported immediately with evidence. Unsportsmanlike conduct may result in removal from events.

Contact

Use the staff channel for urgent issues. For privacy-sensitive incidents, message a staff member directly.
